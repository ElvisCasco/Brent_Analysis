Table~\ref{tab:donorweights} reports the per-donor diagnostics behind the donor-weights
subsection (Section~\ref{sec:results}). For each donor and event: the consensus weight
$\bar w$ (mean normalised $|$weight$|$ across the four comparable models, Bayesian ridge
excluded), the across-model dispersion $\sigma_w$, the pre-window trend correlation
$\rho_{\text{lvl}}=\mathrm{corr}(\log\text{Brent},\log\text{donor})$, the variance
correlation $\rho_{\text{ret}}=\mathrm{corr}(\Delta\log\text{Brent},\Delta\log\text{donor})$,
and the standardised post-window idiosyncratic divergence ``div'' (cumulative residual of
the donor's standardised return on a leave-one-out common factor, Brent-free). All are
descriptive. Generated by \texttt{scripts/donor\_contribution\_analysis.py}.

This table backs the role figure in the main text (Figure~\ref{fig:donorroles}). Trend
carriers are read off the consensus weight $\bar w$ rather than the level correlation
$\rho_{\text{lvl}}$, since a linear correlation misses donors that carry weight through more
than straight-line co-movement, such as the Hormuz yen ($\bar w=0.21$ with $\rho_{\text{lvl}}$
only $0.22$). Late divergers are read off the div column. The $\rho_{\text{ret}}$ column
explains why no variance-tracker role appears in that figure. Its largest value is about
$0.30$ for Russia and $0.17$ for Hormuz, too low for any donor to reproduce Brent's daily
fluctuation, so the category has no members.
